# 🎯 Daily Prep — Live Coaching Track

A running, personalized interview-prep loop layered on top of the static question banks in this repo.

**Candidate profile:** Strong SWE background + 18 months hands-on pretraining experience.
Likely shape: training-infrastructure strength, with modeling/research depth to be calibrated.
**Target role:** Dual track, 50/50 — Forward Deployed AI Engineer **and** Pretraining Engineer.
Both are live paths; 18 months of pretraining is a real, hard-to-hire credential, so we do not
discount that track. Weighting will shift based on ledger data, not assumptions.
**Cadence:** Concept drop each morning (~7am PT) and evening (~7pm PT). Interview questions throughout the day.

## The Loop

```
Morning concept drop  →  interview question  →  your answer
        ↑                                            ↓
   weakness ledger  ←  graded feedback  ←  ──────────┘
```

1. **Concept drop** — a focused reading block. Not a textbook chapter; the 20% that gets asked about.
2. **Question** — posed the way a real interviewer poses it: ambiguous, follow-up-laden, no multiple choice.
3. **Answer** — you respond in your own words, out loud if you can.
4. **Feedback** — graded against a real hiring bar, with the model answer and the follow-ups you'd have gotten.
5. **Ledger update** — weak areas get logged, scored, and re-tested until they're strong.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [WEAKNESS_LEDGER.md](WEAKNESS_LEDGER.md) | Running scorecard of weak areas. The core artifact — drives what gets asked next. |
| [SESSION_LOG.md](SESSION_LOG.md) | Dated log of every concept drop, question, answer grade, and follow-up. |
| [concepts/](concepts/) | The concept drops themselves, one file per day. |

## Grading Scale

Calibrated to a frontier-lab / MAANG hiring bar, not a self-study bar.

| Grade | Meaning | Interview outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **4 — Strong Hire** | Correct, well-structured, volunteers trade-offs and failure modes unprompted. Teaches the interviewer something. | Clear pass |
| **3 — Hire** | Correct and complete. Handles follow-ups. Doesn't go beyond what was asked. | Pass |
| **2 — Mixed** | Right direction, gaps in mechanism or precision. Needs prompting to reach the answer. | Borderline — usually a no at senior level |
| **1 — No Hire** | Wrong, hand-wavy, or pattern-matched a memorized answer onto the wrong question. | Fail |

A topic is **retired** from the ledger after two consecutive 3+ grades on different question framings.
